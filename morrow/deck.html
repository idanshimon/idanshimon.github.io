Which saved note reaches the AI, in 3 stages

From word matching → Spotlight → Bouncer → Newer small AI
Idan ShimonMicrosoft · October 2026
Morrow saves short notes, called lessons, and adds the right ones to the AI's prompt. This is the story of how we chose which note gets in, told in three stages, with real replies from our test runs.

Stage one is the Spotlight. It turns the question and each note into numbers and lets in anything close enough. Here the question mentions a greeting, so the greeting note gets in. The small model follows it and opens with "Good day, Captain Maple." Wrong answer. The big model ignored the extra note and answered all 36 correctly.

Stage two is the Bouncer. Each note gets a small filter trained with yes and no examples. It kept both notes out here, which is right. The small model still answered "The language name is French." instead of "French", so it is still marked wrong. Overall the small model went from 15 to 18 out of 36.
