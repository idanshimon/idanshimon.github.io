## Why
Links shared on LinkedIn and other sites showed a bare gray title card because most pages had no preview image or preview tags.

## What Changes
- Every listed page gets Open Graph and Twitter preview tags with a 1200x627 image.
- One preview image per article in its folder (`og.png`); the homepage uses `assets/og-home.png`.
- Images are rendered from HTML with self-hosted fonts and use the article's real title and numbers.

## Impact
- Head tags on the homepage and every listed article. No visible page change.
- `memory-architecture-comparison/` is unlisted and left as is.
