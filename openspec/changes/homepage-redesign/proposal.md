## Why
The homepage was a single column of text. It did not show what makes the blog different (repeated runs, published bad numbers, checked claims), and it was not fun to scan.

## What Changes
- New hero: "I run the benchmark three times." with an animated panel that replays all 432 workingset trials, one square each, with the 4 trap failures in red.
- Three principles strip.
- Article grid with topic filters, one meaningful drawing per article, key-number badge, reading time and companion links. Newest article is a large card.
- About box and a footer that keeps the no-tracking promise.
- Social preview tags.

## Impact
- `index.html` only. Article pages unchanged.
- No new external dependencies; fonts stay self-hosted.
